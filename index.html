About Me

I'm switching careers and jumping into web development head-first. I can lead teams, adapt to anything, and solve problems fast when things get chaotic. What drives me is actually building something that works—something real that people can use and interact with.

I'm obsessed with building web apps and interactive experiences. Right now, I'm working on a D&D VTT (Virtual Tabletop) web extension and building out a dark fantasy world to go with it. I love mixing storytelling with code—taking creative ideas and turning them into working software that people interact with.

Here's my learning approach: I'm learning from both ends and meeting in the middle. I'm building the web extension from scratch, learning from AI and adapting as I go—understanding how each element actually gets built and why it works. At the same time, I'm taking HTML, CSS, and JavaScript in college, learning the fundamentals and theory. So I'm not just following tutorials blindly—I'm connecting what I'm learning in class to what I'm actually building, and vice versa. That's where the real understanding happens.

My networking classes were honestly a game-changer for understanding how systems actually talk to each other. That foundation really clicked things into place for how web apps work together. But I'm not gonna lie—learning new coding languages is hard for me. Syntax doesn't just click. I have to practice, build actual projects, and push through the frustration. That's just how I learn.

Every bug I hit on the extension, I connect back to what I'm learning in class. Every concept I learn in class, I figure out how to apply to the extension. It's not the fastest way to learn, but it's real, and it actually sticks.
